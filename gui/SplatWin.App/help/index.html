SPLAT! for Windows help

SPLAT! for Windows predicts how a radio signal travels over real terrain: between two stations (path analysis), or from one transmitter over the area around it (coverage maps). It runs the unchanged SPLAT! program by John Magliacane, KD2BD, so its answers are SPLAT!'s answers. The app gives SPLAT! a window: stations by call sign, terrain fetched for you, maps shown over a street map, and files for Google Earth.

New here?

Start with Getting started: add your station, analyze a path, make a coverage map. The first time the app opens it offers a short setup that does the same.

Contents

  • Getting started: step by step, from an empty app to a first map.
  • Stations: adding them by call sign, address, grid, coordinates or the map; importing and exporting .qth files; antenna patterns.
  • Radio settings: frequency, ERP and the other values, in plain words.
  • Path analysis: one transmitter to one receiver, the summary, obstructions and the graphs.
  • The 3D path view: the terrain along a path in 3D, seen from either antenna: what blocks it, and where.
  • Coverage maps: what each map shows and how to read the color key.
  • Terrain: where the terrain comes from, the terrain folder, high-definition terrain and obstacle files.
  • Google Earth: Google Earth Pro and Google Earth on the web.
  • Job folders: what every run leaves behind, and running it again from the command line.
  • Settings and QRZ.com.
  • Troubleshooting: every message the app can show, and what to do.
  • Glossary: dBµV/m, ERP, Fresnel zone and the rest.
  • About: version, licenses and credits.

Finding things in help

  • Contents lists the pages; Index lists topics A to Z; Search finds pages holding all the words you type.
  • Press F1 anywhere in the app to open the help for what you're looking at.
  • Buttons marked What does this mean? open the help for the thing beside them.
  • For a message the app showed you, search for a few of its words, or look in Troubleshooting.

The help works without an internet connection. The pages are in the help folder beside the app, and open in any web browser too.
