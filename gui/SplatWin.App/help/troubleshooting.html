Every message the app can show, grouped by where you'd see it, with what it means and what to do. To find one, search help for a few of its words. Words in italics stand for what the app fills in (a name, a number, a file).

When SPLAT! itself stops with an error, its own output is in splat-log.txt in the job folder; the message gives the path.
