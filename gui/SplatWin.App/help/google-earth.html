Google Earth

Open in Google Earth Pro (top of the window) opens the coverage map or the path you're looking at, whichever you showed last, in Google Earth Pro.

What's in the file

  • A coverage map: the map image laid over the ground, SPLAT!'s color key in the corner, the transmitter, and your other stations on the map as pins in a Stations folder.
  • A path: both stations, the path and its line-of-sight part, with SPLAT!'s descriptions (positions, distance and direction).

The app makes the file (.kmz) from SPLAT!'s own .kml, which it leaves unchanged in the job folder. Two things are put right in the app's copy: SPLAT!'s placemark icon exists only inside Google Earth Pro (the web version refuses files that use it), and SPLAT!'s path file garbles the degree sign.

Google Earth on the web

Without Google Earth Pro, the same file opens in Google Earth in a web browser. Click Google Earth on the web… for the steps:

  1. Open earth.google.com opens it in your browser. Sign in to Google if it asks.
  2. In Google Earth, open Projects, then New project, then Import KML file from computer.
  3. Choose the file: Copy its path and paste it into the file box, or Show the file.

The app never uploads anything itself. Google's menus may change; the import is in Projects.

If Google Earth Pro isn't installed and no program opens .kmz files, Open in Google Earth Pro shows these steps instead. Settings says whether Google Earth Pro was found.
