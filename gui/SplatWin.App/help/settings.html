Settings and QRZ.com

Settings

Settings… (top right) holds:

  • Terrain: the terrain folder in use, with Change… and Use the app's own folder. See Terrain.
  • New coverage maps start with: units, radius, receiver antenna height and terrain shading. These fill in the coverage panel the first time it opens in a session; after that it keeps what you typed.
  • Map: how opaque coverage maps are over the background map (the slider at the top of the window changes it for the moment).
  • QRZ.com: who you're signed in as, and Sign in or out….
  • Google Earth: whether Google Earth Pro was found.
  • Job folders: where jobs go, and Open.

Settings are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SPLATforWindows\settings.json, the stations in Documents\SPLAT\stations.json.

QRZ.com sign-in

Call-sign lookups use QRZ.com when you're signed in: call signs worldwide, and the position the owner set, when they did. Positions need a QRZ XML Logbook Data subscription; without one QRZ gives no positions. Without a sign-in, US call signs are looked up free on callook.info (FCC data).

  • QRZ.com: sign in… (top right): type your QRZ.com user name (usually your call sign) and password, and click Sign in.
  • You type the password; the app never does. It is kept in Windows Credential Manager, encrypted for your Windows account, and never written to a file or a log.
  • Sign out removes it from Credential Manager.
A call sign's position on QRZ.com or callook.info is often the license's mailing address, not the antenna, and for a club or repeater it may be the trustee's home. The app says where each position came from; always check the pin.
