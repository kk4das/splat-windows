QRZ.com replies used by the tests. They are NOT recordings of live sessions (Code never signs in
with the owner's password). They are the example replies in QRZ's own XML specification, version
1.34 (https://www.qrz.com/docs/xml/current_spec.html), with the xmlns attribute that real replies
carry and the spec describes, and with the email and year-of-birth fields left out. The variants
(-geoloc-*, -nonsub, -refused, -badpass) are made from those examples by changing one field each.
